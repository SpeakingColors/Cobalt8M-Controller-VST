# Intro
Cobalt8M Controller sends and receives the MIDI messages (CC and NRPN) of the Modal Cobalt8M, so you can control the synth from your DAW, automate it, and see its settings change in the plugin. It is an early build. The window is the design from docs/mockups, drawn natively: six pages (Preset Manager, Modulation, FX, Sequencer, Keyboard, Settings) with the same layout, knobs and wood sides.

# Quick start
- On the Modal Cobalt8M, turn on MIDI CC receive and transmit (and NRPN where the device has the option).
- Insert the plugin on a MIDI track whose output goes to the Modal Cobalt8M, and send the track's MIDI to the device. Set the plugin's MIDI channel to the one the device listens on.
- Turn a knob in the window (drag up or down; hold Shift for fine steps; double click resets it; the mouse wheel steps it): the matching CC is sent to the device. Turn a knob on the device: the knob moves in the plugin.
- Click a preset in the Preset Manager: the plugin sends a bank change (the Bank button you picked, 0 to 4) then a program change, as the manual describes. The list shows the presets 030 to 079 of the design; scrolling to other presets is not built yet.
- The piano on the Keyboard page plays notes on the device (MIDI channel as set in the plugin).
- Tabs switch the page; the window changes height to fit the page and keeps its shape. Double click a wood side to return to 100%. The (i) button has the info bubbles switch.
- Every control with a documented CC is an automatable parameter in your DAW (104 of them).

# Honest status
- The map has 104 entries (CC chart). It was written from the maker's documentation, nothing was invented.
- None of the 104 controls has been tested on the real device. Every control is marked "not tested on hardware yet" in the list. A range that the documentation does not print is assumed to be 0 to 127 and is marked that way.
- No SysEx and no patch librarian yet. Nothing is sent that is not in the map.
- Incoming MIDI is passed on to the output (pass-through is on). Mapped CCs also move the knobs. The loop guard drops a message that is identical to one the plugin sent in the last 30 ms, which is how an echo from the synth's USB soft thru looks. At most 16 values are sent per audio block so the device is not flooded.
- Controls without a documented MIDI control (for example the FX knobs, the modulation matrix sources and destinations, the sequencer grid, the filter type list, Width and Vintage) are drawn as in the design and keep their state in your project, but send nothing to the device. Their info bubble says so. They need MIDI captures or SysEx documentation first.
- The envelope drawings follow the four envelope knobs. The filter curve, waveforms and LFO shapes are drawings from the design and do not follow the knobs yet.

# Window
- Resize by dragging any edge or corner; the shape stays the same. Hover a control for about a second for its address (CC or NRPN number) and whether it was tested on the device.
- Free and open source (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
